# Ventures

Notes from the investing side of the desk.

I scout early-stage AI companies for [ellipsis Ventures](https://ellipsis-venture.com/) across the UK, EU and US, which mostly means reading a lot of decks and trying to work out which of them describe a company that could still exist in five years. This is where I write down how that judgement actually gets made, and what I get wrong.

Also: healthcare ventures, the slow business of building something useful inside a health system, and what medicine and entrepreneurship genuinely have in common (more than people think, less than the LinkedIn crowd would have you believe).
